# Product Overview — DSP AI Indicator

**Tagline:** Complex Analysis. Simple Decisions.  
**Mode:** Research Mode (educational research — not personalised investment advice)

## What it is

DSP AI Indicator is an institutional-style research platform that surfaces company analysis, explainability, valuation transparency, and related research workspaces through a thin web client over a frozen `/api/v1` API.

## Who it is for

- Independent analysts and learners (Research edition)
- Boutique research desks (Professional)
- Institutions needing SSO and contracted support (Enterprise)

## What it is not

- A brokerage or order-routing system
- Personalised investment advice or a SEBI-registered advice product (unless separately contracted and flagged)
- A browser-side scoring/valuation engine — all analytical reasoning stays server-side

## Trust principles

Traceable · Explainable · Consistent · Actionable · Honest · Transparent AI · Research first
