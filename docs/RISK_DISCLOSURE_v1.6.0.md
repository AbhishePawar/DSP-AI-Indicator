# Risk Disclosure — DSP Platform Web 1.6.0

**Effective:** 2026-07-28

Instruments can decline in value. Liquidity, volatility, currency, and geopolitical events can amplify losses.

## Model and data risk

Models may be wrong, incomplete, delayed, or based on unavailable inputs. Confidence scores are not guarantees. Providers may revise filings or quotes.

## Operational risk

Interruptions, API errors, or cache staleness may delay or prevent report generation.

## Regulatory and jurisdiction risk

Feature availability may depend on research-mode / compliance flags and operator configuration.
