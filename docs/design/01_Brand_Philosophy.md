# 01 — Brand Philosophy

**System:** DSP AI Indicator Institutional Design System  
**Programme:** P9.0 · EPIC-001  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved  

---

## 1. Purpose

Define the institutional brand philosophy that governs how DSP AI Indicator should feel, speak, and appear across every interface.

---

## 2. Brand Essence

DSP AI Indicator is a **quiet institutional research desk** — not a tip app, not a trading arcade, not a neon AI spectacle.

| Pillar | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Trust first | Visual polish never outranks honesty |
| Evidence before opinion | Surfaces privilege sources, categories, and uncertainty |
| Calm clarity | Hierarchy is clear; ornament is rare |
| Human governance | AI assists; humans oversee |
| Long horizon | Design for durable institutions, not trend cycles |

---

## 3. Emotional Contract

Users should feel:

- Oriented (they know where they are)
- Informed (they know what is known vs estimated)
- Respected (no hype, no false certainty)
- Able to act next (investigation steps are always present)

Users must never feel:

- Pressured to trade
- Confused by competing accents
- Misled by color-as-recommendation
- Overwhelmed by dashboard noise in the first viewport of branded pages

---

## 4. Visual Character

| Attribute | Spec |
|---|---|
| Palette character | Teal / slate on warm paper (light) and deep institutional green-slate (dark) |
| Typography character | Fraunces (display) + Sora (UI/body) |
| Surface philosophy | Flat + border by default; elevation sparingly |
| Motion character | Explains state; never celebrates |
| Density | Research-readable; progressive disclosure |

**Forbidden brand directions:** purple-on-white AI gradients; terracotta-on-cream generic AI landing tropes; broadsheet hairline newspaper pastiche as default chrome; neon glow stacks.

---

## 5. Product Promise (Brand Language)

| Primary | Complex Analysis. Simple Decisions. |
|---|---|
| Secondary | Professional Investment Research for Everyone. |

These lines are brand signals — not chrome clutter. On marketing surfaces, brand may be hero-level; in research workspaces, brand yields to content hierarchy.

---

## 6. Relationship to Trust & Ontology

Brand expression must preserve:

1. User Trust Standard (traceable, explainable, consistent, actionable, honest)
2. Research Mode vocabulary unless SEBI-gated recommendation flags unlock
3. REP-002 meanings (UI labels must not invent parallel terms)

---

## 7. Success Test

If the first viewport of a branded page could belong to another product after removing the nav, branding is too weak.  
If a research screen could be mistaken for a tip app after removing labels, branding has failed.
