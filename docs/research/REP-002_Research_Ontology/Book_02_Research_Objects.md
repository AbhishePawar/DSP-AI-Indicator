# Book 02 — Research Objects

## Purpose

Define the enduring research artefacts that DSP AI Indicator creates, stores, references, and revisits over time.

## Scope

Named research objects and their meaning as institutional artefacts, independent of storage technology, API shape, or user-interface presentation.

## Relationship to Book 01

Book 02 consumes Book 01 — Core Principles. Every research object must preserve the distinctions among Truth, Evidence, Fact, Observation, Assumption, Inference, and Confidence. Book 02 does not redefine Book 01 concepts.

## Dependencies

- Book 01 — Core Principles
