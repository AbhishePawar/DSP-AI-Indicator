# User Experience Guidelines

**Epic:** PR1.0 · pairs with [PRODUCT_DESIGN_STANDARD_V2.md](PRODUCT_DESIGN_STANDARD_V2.md)

---

## Experience pillars

1. **Summary first** — Research Conclusion and Executive Summary before detail.  
2. **Details later** — progressive disclosure; deep evidence at the bottom.  
3. **Interpreted charts** — no naked plots; always attach “what this means”.  
4. **Explained metrics** — Metric Card standard everywhere.  
5. **AI on every page** — entry to Copilot + mandatory Challenge Mode on conclusions.  
6. **Tooltips for jargon** — every financial term hover/focus definition.  
7. **Mobile first** — primary flows usable on small screens.  
8. **Accessibility first** — semantic HTML, keyboard, focus, ARIA.

---

## Four-question checklist (ship gate)

Before releasing a screen, verify:

| # | Question | Pass criteria |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is happening? | Clear status / rating / snapshot |
| 2 | Why is it happening? | Causal / evidence explanation |
| 3 | Why should I care? | Investor-relevant consequence |
| 4 | What should I do next? | Takeaway / next navigation |

---

## Research Mode language

Prefer: DSP View, Attractive, Caution, Fairly Valued, Estimated Intrinsic Value
Range, Research Conclusion, Investment Assessment.

Avoid: Buy, Sell, Hold, Target Price, Strong Buy (unless SEBI flags unlock).

---

## Thin client

Browser never values, recommends, or reasons. UX presents API envelopes and
compliance terminology only.
