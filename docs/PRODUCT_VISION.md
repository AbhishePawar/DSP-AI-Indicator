# Product Vision

**Epic:** PR1.0 — Product Strategy & Compliance Architecture  
**Tagline:** Complex Analysis. Simple Decisions.  
**Secondary:** Professional Investment Research for Everyone.

---

## What DSP is

DSP AI Indicator is an **Explainable AI Investment Research Platform**.

It helps investors **understand businesses** before making investment decisions.

It is **not** a stock tip platform.

---

## Two-phase product

| Phase | Identity | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | AI-powered Investment Research & Decision Support Platform | **Active** (Research Mode) |
| **Phase 2** | SEBI Registered AI Equity Research Platform | Architecture prepared; not activated |

---

## Mission

Help investors understand businesses before making investment decisions.

## Design principle (every screen)

1. What is happening?  
2. Why is it happening?  
3. Why should I care?  
4. What should I do next?

A screen that fails any of these four questions is incomplete.

---

## Product philosophy

- Summary first, details later  
- Every metric explained in plain English  
- Every chart interpreted  
- Every page assisted by AI (backend copilot)  
- Progressive disclosure  
- Mobile first · Accessibility first  
- Thin client — intelligence from frozen `/api/v1` only
