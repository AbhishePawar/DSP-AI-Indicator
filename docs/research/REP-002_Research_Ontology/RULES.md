## REP-002 — Research Ontology

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Approved

Owner: DSP Research Team

Governed By:
DSP Research Constitution v1.0

---

## Rules

1. Every concept has exactly one official definition.

2. Every concept belongs to one primary ontology book.

3. Concepts may reference other books but never redefine them.

4. Every concept follows the official ontology template.

5. Every modification must be recorded in CHANGELOG.md.

6. Definitions must be implementation-independent.

7. The ontology defines meaning, not algorithms.

8. Future platform modules must consume this ontology rather than create independent definitions.
