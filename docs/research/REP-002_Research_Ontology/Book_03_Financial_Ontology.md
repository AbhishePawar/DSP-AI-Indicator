# Book 03 — Financial Ontology

## Purpose

Define the institutional financial vocabulary used consistently across valuation, business quality assessment, ratio analysis, portfolio intelligence, reporting, and explainability within DSP AI Indicator.

## Scope

Statement elements, financial condition concepts, and financial performance meanings as ontology definitions. This book does not define calculation algorithms, scoring formulas, engine implementations, API contracts, or user-interface presentation.

## Relationship to Prior Books

Book 03 consumes Book 01 — Core Principles and Book 02 — Research Objects. Financial concepts must preserve distinctions among Truth, Evidence, Fact, Observation, Assumption, Inference, and Confidence, and must remain anchored to research objects such as Organization, Security, Financial Statement, Metric, Time Period, and Currency. Book 03 does not redefine Book 01 or Book 02 concepts.

# Core Definition

## Definition

Revenue is the gross inflow of economic benefits arising from ordinary activities of an organization during a defined reporting period, recognized according to applicable accounting standards. Within DSP AI Indicator, revenue is a foundational financial concept used to evaluate scale, growth, and the top-line economic activity of organizations and securities.

## Purpose

Revenue provides the primary measure of commercial activity from which profitability, efficiency, and many valuation relationships are derived.

## Why It Matters

Nearly every financial and valuation workflow depends on a coherent understanding of revenue. Correctly defining revenue enables consistent growth analysis, margin construction, peer comparison, and transparent explanation of analytical conclusions.

---

# Characteristics

## Characteristics

Revenue should be:

- Period-specific
- Recognized under stated accounting standards
- Distinguishable from other income where material
- Traceable to financial statements
- Comparable across reporting periods when restated consistently
- Denominated in an explicit currency

## What It Is Not

Revenue is not:

- Operating profit
- Free cash flow
- Capital expenditure
- A market price
- A valuation multiple
- An investment recommendation

---

# Evidence

## Evidence Requirements

Revenue definitions and values should be supported by:

- Audited or reviewed financial statements
- Regulatory filings
- Accounting policy disclosures
- Segment disclosures where relevant
- Restatement notes when applicable
- Verified financial databases

## Confidence Drivers

Confidence increases when:

- Recognition policies are disclosed.
- Audited statements are available.
- Restatements are transparent.
- Segment composition is clear.
- Independent sources agree.

## Validation

Revenue is validated through:

- Statement line-item verification
- Period reconciliation
- Currency confirmation
- Restatement consistency checks
- Source confirmation
- Peer-definition comparison where relevant

# Research Guidance

## Research Implication

Analytical engines and research reports within DSP AI Indicator shall treat revenue as a defined financial concept with explicit period, currency, and source attribution so that growth and profitability conclusions remain auditable.

## Examples

Examples include:

- Net sales
- Service revenue
- Subscription revenue
- Product revenue
- Interest income for banks where classified as revenue under applicable standards
- Gross premiums for insurers where classified as revenue under applicable standards

## Limitations

Revenue measures recognized commercial activity only. It does not establish profitability, cash generation, earnings quality, or intrinsic value without further financial analysis.

# Core Definition

## Definition

Operating profit is the profit generated from an organization’s core operating activities after operating expenses and before financing costs, taxes, and non-operating items as presented under applicable reporting conventions. Within DSP AI Indicator, operating profit is a core financial concept used to assess operating earnings power independent of capital structure.

## Purpose

Operating profit provides a measure of core business profitability required to evaluate operating efficiency, earnings durability, and operating margin quality.

## Why It Matters

Valuation, business quality, and financial strength assessments depend on distinguishing operating earnings from financing and non-operating effects. Correctly defining operating profit enables consistent margin analysis and transparent explanation of operating performance.

---

Operating profit should be:

- Derived from operating activities
- Period-specific
- Separated from financing and tax effects where presentation allows
- Traceable to financial statements
- Comparable across periods under consistent classification
- Explicit about adjustments when normalized

## What It Is Not

Operating profit is not:

- Revenue
- Free cash flow
- Net income
- Return on equity
- A market valuation
- An investment recommendation

Working capital is the capital tied to short-term operating assets and liabilities required to run the business day to day, commonly expressed through relationships among receivables, inventories, payables, and related operating balances. Within DSP AI Indicator, working capital is a core financial concept used to assess liquidity needs, cash conversion, and operating efficiency.

## Purpose

Working capital provides the short-cycle capital measure required to understand operating liquidity and the cash absorbed or released by ordinary business operations.

## Why It Matters

Cash flow quality and operational efficiency depend on working capital behavior. Correct definition enables consistent analysis of cash conversion, seasonality, and the sustainability of reported earnings.
