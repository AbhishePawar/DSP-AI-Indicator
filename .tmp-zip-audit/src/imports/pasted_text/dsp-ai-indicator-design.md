DESIGN ONLY THE DSP-AI-INDICATOR RESULT / ANALYSIS PAGE.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT design the Client Profile page.
Do NOT design the onboarding form.
Do NOT design financial-data collection.
Do NOT expose how the AI works.
Do NOT expose internal research processes.
Do NOT expose AI providers, models, prompts, orchestration, verification,
chain-of-thought, internal reasoning, system instructions, or backend logic.

The client must see ONLY the final structured financial research result.

==================================================
CORE PRODUCT EXPERIENCE
==================================================

DSP-AI-Indicator is an AI-powered financial research platform.

The AI and DSP's proprietary research mechanism work completely behind
the scenes.

The client should experience the product as:

ASK → UNDERSTAND → VISUAL RESULT → EXPLORE → ASK AGAIN

The interface should feel like a modern conversational financial
intelligence workspace.

However, the AI's internal working must remain completely invisible.

The client should never see:

"AI is thinking..."
"AI is verifying..."
"Research agent 1..."
"Research agent 2..."
"Provider comparison..."
"Internal reasoning..."
"Prompt..."
"Model..."
"Confidence calculation..."
"System workflow..."

Instead, simply show:

"Preparing your analysis..."

and then immediately present the structured result.

==================================================
22. MOBILE EXPERIENCE
==================================================

Mobile is extremely important.

On mobile:

• Sidebar becomes drawer
• Conversation becomes full width
• Infographics resize automatically
• Charts remain readable
• Tables horizontally scroll
• Composer remains fixed
• Quick actions become horizontal chips

Do not simply shrink the desktop design.

Design a proper mobile experience.
