# 02 — Brand Guidelines

**System:** DSP AI Indicator Institutional Design System  
**Programme:** P9.0 · EPIC-001  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved  

---

## 1. Purpose

Operational rules for brand expression: naming, voice, wordmark, imagery, and prohibited patterns.

---

## 2. Naming

| Context | Form |
|---|---|
| Full product | DSP AI Indicator |
| Short | DSP |
| Platform | DSP Platform |
| Ontology | REP-002 (never “the AI dictionary” in UI) |

Do not invent alternate product nicknames in chrome.

---

## 3. Voice & Tone

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| Calm, plain English | Hype (“guaranteed”, “can’t miss”) |
| Evidence-first | Tip language in Research Mode |
| Disclose uncertainty | Fake precision |
| Institutional vocabulary aligned to REP-002 | Synonym sprawl (“moat” redefined locally) |
| Actionable next investigation | Vague “stay tuned” endings |

**Research Mode:** present ratings and investigation language — not BUY / SELL / HOLD / Target Price chrome unless feature flags unlock SEBI-compliant recommendation presentation.

---

## 4. Wordmark & Logo

| Rule | Spec |
|---|---|
| Wordmark type | Fraunces, weight 500 |
| Color | `--fg` on `--bg`; inverse on accent only when needed |
| Clear space | ≥ 0.5× cap-height around wordmark |
| Minimum size | 14px cap height digital; avoid sub-pixel blur |
| Lockups | Prefer text wordmark; icon mark only if approved asset exists |

Do not place the wordmark inside glassmorphic blobs or animated neon frames.

---

## 5. Imagery

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---|---|
| Product UI in calm states | Stock-trader high-five stock photos |
| Abstract institutional paper/teal washes (subtle) | Purple nebula AI art as brand |
| Charts with captions | Unlabelled sparkline decoration |
| Real research context | Memes, emoji clusters as brand |

Marketing heroes: full-bleed atmospheric plane when used; no floating badge stickers on hero media (see frontend brand rules).

---

## 6. Co-branding & Third Parties

Data provider and regulatory marks appear as attribution, never as competing heroes. Always keep DSP brand hierarchy primary on DSP-owned surfaces.

---

## 7. Do / Don’t Summary

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| One primary CTA accent | Competing neon CTAs |
| Text + color for meaning | Color-only “Sell” red |
| Quiet confidence | FOMO banners |
| Consistent Research Mode labels | Brokerage slang in research UI |
