# Privacy Policy — DSP Platform Web 1.6.0

**Effective:** 2026-07-28  
**Status:** Operational product summary (not a substitute for jurisdictional counsel)

## Overview

DSP AI Indicator processes account credentials, session tokens, research request metadata, and optional browser-stored preferences to deliver research intelligence.

## Data we collect

- Account and authentication data (username, display name, email when provided, roles, session tokens)
- Research usage metadata (tickers, timestamps, correlation IDs, UI preferences)
- Optional local beta feedback (redacted; must not include envelopes, holdings, or secrets)
- Operator-controlled server logs (avoid unnecessary PII)

## How we use data

Authenticate users, authorize research endpoints, display frozen `/api/v1` outputs, persist optional UI preferences, and support reliability/security operations. The browser does not run valuation or recommendation engines.

## Cookies and local storage

First-party storage for session continuity, theme/preferences, research disclaimer acknowledgement, and optional feedback drafts. See Cookie Policy.

## Sharing

Requests go to the configured DSP API. DSP does not sell personal data. Infrastructure subprocessors are controlled by the deploying organization.

## Retention

Browser data until cleared by the user. Server retention follows the operator’s policy.

## Your rights

- **Account management** — Profile / Settings where enabled
- **Data access** — request account and research-metadata summaries from your operator
- **Data deletion** — request deletion; clear browser storage locally
- **Contact** — `privacy@dsp-ai-indicator.example` (operators may substitute a production address)
- **Complaints** — escalate via your organization’s compliance channel, then the contact above
