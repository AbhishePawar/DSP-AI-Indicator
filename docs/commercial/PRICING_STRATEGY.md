# Pricing Strategy — DSP AI Indicator

**Epic:** P6.1 · Indicative commercial terms for Research Mode product.  
**Not legal advice.** Final MSAs / order forms supersede this summary.

## Pricing model

- **Research:** Free tier with hard monthly usage caps.
- **Professional:** Seat-based subscription (monthly or annual). Annual = ~2 months free vs monthly.
- **Enterprise:** Custom quote (SSO, dedicated support, capacity, DPA, SSO/IdP).

Currency for list prices: **USD**. Local currency invoicing available under Enterprise.

## Trial policy

| Edition | Trial |
|---|---|
| Research | N/A (always free with limits) |
| Professional | 14 days · full Professional features · card optional until day 14 |
| Enterprise | Up to 30 days · scoped sandbox · requires sales approval |

Trials do not waive Research Mode / disclaimer acknowledgements.

## Subscription terms

- Billing cycle: monthly or annual (Professional); Enterprise per contract.
- Auto-renewal unless cancelled ≥7 days before renewal (Professional).
- Taxes: billed as applicable by jurisdiction.
- Seat changes: pro-rated on Professional; Enterprise via change order.

## Upgrade / downgrade

1. Contact `sales@dsp-ai-indicator.example` (or in-app Support → Sales).
2. Upgrade: effective immediately after confirmation; unused prepaid time credited where applicable.
3. Downgrade: effective next billing cycle; overage seats must be removed first.
4. Edition feature flags / invite allowlists updated by operators after commercial confirmation.

No self-serve billing console is required for P6.1 RC — operator-assisted changes are the supported path.

## Refund policy

- **Professional annual:** unused prepaid period may be refunded within **14 days** of purchase if Enterprise features were not provisioned and usage remained within fair-use audit.
- **Monthly Professional / Research:** no cash refund; cancel to stop renewal.
- **Enterprise:** per MSA; typically no refund of prepaid periods after go-live.
- Chargebacks / abuse: investigated; accounts may be suspended pending review.

## Research / advice boundary

All paid and free editions remain **research and educational** unless a separately regulated product is contracted. Pricing does not imply personalised investment advice.
