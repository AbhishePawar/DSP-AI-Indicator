# Epic V2.0 Sprint 2 — Client Management Workspace

**Web:** `2.0.0`

## Mission

Presentation-only client management on the Advisor foundation. No backend, auth, or persistence.

## Delivered

- Client Directory — search, filter (risk / review / size), sort (alphabet, activity, meeting due, …)
- Expanded Client Profile — dashboard, objectives, risk, meetings, tasks, notes, research history, documents placeholder
- Meeting Timeline — upcoming / completed / cancelled + review notes + action items
- Kanban Task Board — To Do · In Progress · Waiting · Completed (priority, due, owner)
- Client Notes — pinned / meeting / research / advisor (demo)
- Research History timeline — companies, exports, portfolio reviews, saved research

## Enable

`NEXT_PUBLIC_ADVISOR_DEMO=true`

## Trust

Client layer never mutates research conclusions, evidence, confidence, methodology, or decision traces.

## Non-goals

Auth · Persistence · CRM · Email · Calendar · Broker · Trading · Tax
